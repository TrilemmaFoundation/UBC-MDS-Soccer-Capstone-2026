We built a fully automated football analytics platform for the Trilemma Foundation on top of the StatsBomb open event dataset [@statsbomb2024]. The platform ingests raw match, event, and lineup data from 16 competitions (3,464 matches, approximately 12.2 million on-ball events) into Google BigQuery, transforms it through a three-layer dbt pipeline into analytics-ready tables, enriches those tables with two machine learning models, and serves the results through a Looker Studio dashboard and a plain-English chatbot.

Three research questions were addressed. For RQ1, unsupervised clustering (PCA + K-Means, k = 5) on 13 per-90 features identified five interpretable outfield archetypes — Low Activity, Defensive Anchor, Creative Playmaker, Pressing Forward, and Creative Winger — across 5,759 player-seasons. Tactical role assignment is driven primarily by position, with league context producing secondary but detectable differences in archetype mix. For RQ2, 489 dual-context players (those with ≥ 270 minutes in both club and international football) were scored for cross-context consistency; 32% qualify as Elite performers in both settings, while 36% are single-context specialists. For RQ3, a Django-based chatbot powered by a Groq LLM translates natural language questions into BigQuery SQL and returns formatted answers in under ten seconds, making the full dataset queryable without SQL knowledge.

The delivered product is reproducible, fully documented, and deployable with a single `docker compose up` command.
