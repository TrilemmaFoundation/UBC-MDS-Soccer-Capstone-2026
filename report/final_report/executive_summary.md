We built a fully automated football analytics platform for the Trilemma Foundation on top of the StatsBomb open event dataset [@statsbomb2024]. The platform ingests raw match, event, and lineup data from 16 competitions (3,464 matches, approximately 12.2 million on-ball events) into Google BigQuery, transforms it through a three-layer dbt pipeline into analytics-ready tables, enriches those tables with two machine learning models, and serves the results through a Looker Studio dashboard and a plain-English chatbot.

Three research questions guided the project. **RQ1 (Player archetypes)** asked what tactical roles exist across European football and how they differ by position and competition. Unsupervised clustering (PCA + K-Means, k = 5) on 13 per-90 features produced five outfield archetypes (Low Activity, Defensive Anchor, Creative Playmaker, Pressing Forward, and Creative Winger) across 5,759 player-seasons, with position as the dominant driver and league context secondary. **RQ2 (Cross-context consistency)** asked whether players maintain similar profiles for club and country. Among 489 dual-context players with $\geq 270$ minutes in both settings, 32% rank as Elite in both contexts and 36% as single-context specialists. **RQ3 (Natural language access)** asked whether non-technical analysts could query the dataset without SQL. A Groq-powered Django chatbot returns mart-backed answers in under ten seconds.

The delivered product is reproducible, fully documented, and deployable with a single `docker compose up` command.
